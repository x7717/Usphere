## Key discoveries (documented for future ports)

1. **Still Life's tropical family is unreachable on 26.3.** `tropical_rainforest`,
   `monsoon_forest`, `humid_savanna`, `tropical_highlands` all MISS live — their
   climate temperature windows ([0.66..1]) sit above the maximum of the actual
   temperature field (max ≈ -0.05). Any biome carved from those windows never
   generates. New v2.2 biomes therefore anchor on LIVE donors.
2. **Tie-overtake pattern.** Copies of a live donor's climate entries inserted
   BEFORE the donor win the climate R-tree's stable tie-break (identical boxes,
   earlier leaf wins). `tepui_plateau` takes the central weirdness half of
   `painted_peaks`, `finnish_taiga` takes half of `norwegian_alpine_tundra`;
   both donors remain alive on the edges.
3. **Vanilla merge mechanics.** The vanilla 26.3 grid (7594 entries) is dumped
   from the engine itself (`OverworldBiomeBuilder.addBiomes`, see
   `verification/vanilla_biomes.jsonl`) and appended AFTER the SL entries so SL
   wins ties. Vanilla entries intersecting protected community-biome boxes are
   dropped (791 of 7594) — without that filter, wide vanilla boxes oust
   gap-living biomes like grand_canyon (live regression, caught and fixed).
4. **Feature-order cycles.** The engine builds a global feature order per step
   across all biomes in the source; conflicting pair orders between SL and
   vanilla biomes crash the server (`patch_sugar_cane`/`patch_pumpkin`,
   `fossil_*`/`monster_room`, `emeralds`/`underwater_magma`...). Fix: per-step
   topological canon from the vanilla graph, SL biomes re-sorted vanilla-first,
   plus one targeted emeralds/magma unification. Final graph: 527 nodes,
   topological sort passes, SCC scan = 0 cycles.
5. **26.3 climate codec**: `Climate.ParameterPoint` record order is
   T,H,C,E,depth,weirdness + offset-as-long; `TargetPoint` has 6 longs. The
   multi_noise codec rejects a list-valued offset ("No key preset" parse error).
